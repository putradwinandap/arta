# Product Glossary

## Household
A group of users intentionally managing finances together. It is not restricted to biological family relationships.

## Member
A user participating in a household.

## Wallet
A logical place where money is held, such as cash, a bank account, or an e-wallet.

## Transaction
A financial record that changes or describes money movement. Core transaction semantics currently include income, expense, and transfer.

## Expense
Money leaving a household wallet for consumption or payment. Eligible expenses may consume a budget.

## Income
Money entering the household from an external source.

## Transfer
Movement of money between two wallets belonging to the managed financial context. A transfer is neither household income nor household expense.

## Capture
The act of getting transaction information into Arta, potentially before all details are known.

## Classification
Adding or confirming semantic details such as category, merchant, purpose, or other metadata after capture.

## Transaction Inbox
A review queue for captured transactions that are incomplete, uncertain, or require confirmation.

## Budget
A spending constraint or allocation for a defined period and scope.

## Goal
A target financial outcome with a target amount and measurable progress.

## Reconciliation
Comparing Arta's expected financial state with an independently observed state, such as an actual wallet balance, to identify discrepancies.

## ADR
Architecture Decision Record: a durable record of an important technical or domain decision, its context, and consequences.
